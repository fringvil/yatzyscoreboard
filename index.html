Yatzy Challenge

Track players and totals for a full Yatzy game, with optional betting and digital dice.

Game Setup

Up to 30 characters.

    Pre-Game Bets

    Each player may place multiple bets, but only one bet per category. A bet is tied to a Yatzy category (or to winning the whole game) and a task the loser must complete. Choose "All players" to place the same bet for everyone at once.

      Digital Dice

      Rolls: 0 / 3

      Click a die to hold/release it between rolls. After rolling, click a category's dice icon in the scorecard to apply the current dice score for the active player.
